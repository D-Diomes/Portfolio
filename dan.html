About

The art of

I create digital artworks that explore tension — between order and chaos, light and dark, the handmade and the algorithmic. My practice spans generative code, digital painting, and immersive 3D environments.

Based between Seoul and London, I work with institutions, galleries, and collectors seeking work that challenges the boundary between technology and art.
